### Tier 2

- keff_sphalerite

### Tier 3

- sorption parameters

## Rules

- Do not calibrate trace metals before pH is acceptable.
- Do not change more than 2–3 parameters per run.
- Avoid changes larger than ×3 unless strongly justified.
- Prefer process-based explanations over purely statistical fitting.
