#  Humidity Cell Conceptual Model

## Objective

Simulate drainage chemistry from sulfide-bearing waste rock under humidity-cell conditions.

## System

- Laboratory humidity cell
- Waste rock thickness: 1 m equivalent
- Unsaturated flow
- Oxygen diffusion from top boundary
- Drainage collected at bottom

## Primary sulfides

- Pyrite
- Sphalerite
- Chalcopyrite
- Galena

## Neutralizing minerals

- Calcite
- Dolomite

## Silicates

- Chlorite
- Muscovite
- Plagioclase
- Quartz

## Main processes

1. Oxygen diffusion
2. Sulfide oxidation
3. Acid generation
4. Carbonate buffering
5. Secondary mineral precipitation
6. Sorption

## Expected controls

SO4:
- controlled mainly by pyrite oxidation

pH:
- controlled mainly by calcite and dolomite

Zn:
- controlled mainly by sphalerite dissolution

Mg:
- controlled mainly by chlorite and dolomite dissolution
